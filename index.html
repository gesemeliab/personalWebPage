01 / ABOUTA LITTLE CONTEXT
gm.

Gesem Gudino Mejia Rochester, NY

Curious by nature.
Hands-on by design.

I work at the intersection of robot learning, perception, and control, turning ideas into systems that interact with the physical world.

I'm a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Rochester Institute of Technology and a Graduate Research Assistant in KAS Lab. My research focuses on safe decision-making for embodied agents, self-supervised world models, and uncertainty-aware control.

Before RIT, I spent nearly five years at Intel Labs building multimodal learning systems. My path has taken me from aerial robots and agricultural rovers to industrial AI and underwater autonomy.
